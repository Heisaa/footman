# The reference figures

These are what `art/figure/body.py` is measured against. Four images, supplied
by the owner. The numbers in `body.py` were read off them, and there is no way
to check a change without the picture it came from.

| file | what it is |
| --- | --- |
| `01-moustache-red.png` | Red kit, brown cap of hair, handlebar moustache, white V and cuffs, crest, white shorts with a red side stripe, red socks with two white hoops, black boots with three stripes and studs. Glossy vinyl, seamless off-white background. **The primary reference.** |
| `02-perm-blue.png` | Blue kit, a perm of fat individual lobes carried down past the ears to the jaw, a simple smile groove, white V and cuffs, white shorts, white socks with blue hoops, black boots. Same finish and background. |
| `03-blocky-royal.png` | A harder, more geometric register: rounded-box head, a **square** block nose, a line mouth, small dot eyes, arms as separate cylinders with daylight between them and the body, tapered block boots, hair as a heap of separate balls. |
| `04-four-lads.png` | A rank of four in a softer, fabric register — leader, engine, striker, keeper. Shirt numbers, a green long-sleeved keeper with gloves, slit eyes and a small tan triangular nose. Useful for the **rank**, and for what a squad looks like side by side. |

## What was measured off them

In fractions of total figure height, from image 1:

```
ground 0 | boot top .095 | sock top .225 | shorts hem .245
shirt hem .380 | shoulder .605 | chin .625 | skull top .950
```

head half-width `.167`, trunk half-width `.180`–`.218`. The head being nearly as
wide as the shoulders is most of what makes the register: a head this size on
ordinary shoulders is a caricature, and on these it is a toy.

## What the references agree on, and what carries the look

- **Squat, big-headed, no neck whatever.** The head sits straight on the shirt.
- **Three hard edges and everything else soft**: the shirt hem, the shorts hem
  and the sock top are crisp; every other transition is a fillet. That contrast
  is the kit.
- **The face is moulded, never drawn.** Brows are ridges, the nose is a button
  with a fillet round it, the mouth is a groove. Eyes are solid black shapes
  with no whites and no pupils.
- **A soft travelling highlight**, and dark creases under the chin, in the
  collar, under the sleeve and between the legs. This is doing more work than
  the colour is.
